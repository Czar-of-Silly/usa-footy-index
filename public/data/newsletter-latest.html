September 15, 2026 · 2026 MLS Season
Week after week the standings picture sharpens, and two clubs now hold commanding positions at the top of each conference. Nashville SC lead the East with 54 points from a 16-6-3 record, while Vancouver Whitecaps front the West on 46 points. The individual prize races are equally compelling. Petar Musa of Dallas tops the Golden Boot with 18 goals, though the xG table tells a different story, with Rafael Navarro of St. Louis generating 17.3 expected goals without matching that output in the standings column. The efficiency story of the season belongs to Nicolás Fernández of New York City, who sits 6.6 goals above his expected tally, the widest positive gap in the league and a number that rewards closer inspection this week.
