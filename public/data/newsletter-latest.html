September 22, 2026 · 2026 MLS Season
Week 26 of the MLS season has Nashville SC and Vancouver Whitecaps setting the pace in their respective conferences. Nashville lead the East with 57 points from a 17-6-3 record, while Vancouver top the West on 49 points with a 15-4-6 mark, two clubs building genuine cases for best team in the league. Rafael Navarro of St. Louis leads all players with 17.4 expected goals, suggesting room at the top remains contested. The season's most ruthless finisher is Houston's Guilherme, outperforming his xG by 6.6, a gap that stands alone across the entire league.
